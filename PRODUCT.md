# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Inferred from the original brief (no interview: the user asked for independent decisions without questions). Every fact below is taken from that brief or labelled as an assumption.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React + TypeScript (Vite), simulation engine in a Web Worker, Canvas2D world renderer, Zustand UI store. Chosen for a pure-TS deterministic engine shared by worker, tests and headless soak runs.

## Users

A curious individual (the brief's author) who wants to "act like a god": watch thousands of autonomous creatures live, evolve and build civilizations, and intervene to run experiments. Assumption: sessions are long and exploratory, often left running in the background and revisited.

## Product Purpose

WORLD ZERO is an interactive artificial-world simulator. Success means opening it and immediately feeling like you are looking at a living universe that keeps evolving whether you watch or not, with real emergent behavior rather than scripted animations or faked statistics.

## Positioning

The behavior is genuinely simulated: individual creatures with genomes, needs and memories; species that split and go extinct by measurable divergence; tribes whose technology and diplomacy follow from their members. The interface reports what the simulation actually did.

## Operating Context

Desktop browser, full screen. The user alternates between watching (map, event feed), investigating (inspector, family tree, charts) and intervening (god tools, environment sliders, scenarios). The world autosaves and catches up on time spent away.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Procedural worlds from seeds; 1x/2x/10x/100x speed and pause; god tools on the map; global environment controls; seven preset scenarios plus custom ones; save/load and file export; event chronicle; species evolution tree; civilization stats; creature inspector with follow mode. Simulation must stay responsive with thousands of creatures.

## Brand Commitments

Name: WORLD ZERO. The brief pins the visual direction: "premium scientific simulation interface", "dark futuristic interface", "beautiful atmospheric world rendering", "smooth animations", "subtle glowing details", "elegant typography", "rich data visualizations", "beautiful charts", "responsive layout", "professional transitions"; the world "should be visually captivating, not just a collection of colored dots".

## Evidence on Hand

None external. All creature, species, tribe and event content is generated by the simulation at runtime; nothing about real organisms or users may be claimed.

## Product Principles

1. The world is the protagonist; the interface frames it and never competes with it.
2. Every number shown is computed by the simulation, never decorative.
3. Story over telemetry: events are written as history, and any event can take you to where it happened.
4. Intervention is immediate and visible: every god action has a visible consequence on the map.
5. Deep when asked, quiet when not: detail lives one click away.
